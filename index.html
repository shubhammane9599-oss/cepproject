          Sugarcane • Vegetative Balanced Nutrition Plan

          NPK 19:19:19 + Urea Split Top-Dressing

          Nitrogen (N) - 19% Phosphorus (P) - 19% Potassium (K) - 19%
          🎯 Why it is recommended:
          During the vegetative tillering phase, your crop builds structural stems and expands leaf area. Balanced NPK drives simultaneous root branching and vigorous leafy canopy formation.
          ⏰ When to use it:
          Apply 25 to 45 days after planting when active tillers are emerging. Apply in split doses right before scheduled watering.
          🚜 General Application Guidance:
          For drip systems: Dissolve water-soluble grade 19:19:19 @ 3-4 kg/acre per fertigation run. For soil broadcasting: Incorporate 5-8 cm deep into moist soil to minimize nitrogen volatilization.
          🌱 Bio-Fertilizer & Organic Alternative:
          Supplement with 2 tons of decomposed vermicompost and apply Jeevamrut (200 L/acre) every 2 weeks to unlock trapped soil minerals naturally.
          ⚠️ Agricultural Expert Disclaimer: Exact chemical dosage depends on soil test reports and local climatic conditions. Do not overdose. Always consult your local Agriculture Extension Officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) and strictly follow product label instructions.
          Best Practices

          Comprehensive Crop Growth Recommendations

          Simple, easy-to-understand cultivation practices covering irrigation, soil enrichment, nutrition, and IPM for peak yields.

          💧

          1. Water & Irrigation Management

          Drip irrigation reduces water consumption by 45%. Critical stages include the Formative Stage (60-130 days) and Grand Growth Stage (130-250 days). Maintain soil moisture at 50% available water and avoid waterlogging.

          🪴

          2. Soil Health & Preparation

          Ideal pH is 6.5 to 7.5. Deep well-drained loamy soil with minimum 0.5% organic carbon. Incorporate 10-15 tonnes of FYM or well-rotted vermicompost per acre before furrow opening.

          🧪

          3. Balanced Nutrient Management

          Recommended Dose: 250:115:115 kg N:P:K per hectare for Suru cane. Apply Nitrogen in 4 splits: 10% at planting, 40% at tillering (6-8 weeks), 10% at earthing up, and 40% at grand growth.

          🛡️

          4. Pest & Disease Prevention (IPM)

          Early Shoot Borer: Release Trichogramma chilonis egg parasitoids (20,000/acre). For white grub control, apply Metarhizium anisopliae or neem cake at first monsoon showers.

          💡

          5. Basic Crop-Care & Yield Boosting Tips

          Perform dry mulching with sugarcane trash (5 tonnes/acre) to conserve moisture and suppress 85% weed growth. Timely earthing-up (मोठी बांधणी) prevents cane lodging during stormy weather.
